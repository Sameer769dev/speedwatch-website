Clear answers to common questions about internet speeds, broadband contracts, and latency diagnostics.

SpeedWatch measures raw HTTP chunk throughput directly against high-capacity global CDN edge servers without proxy compression, accurately measuring latency, download speed, and upload throughput matching real-world browsing, streaming, and gaming conditions.
ISP throttling commonly occurs during peak evening hours or on specific traffic types like video streaming and gaming. If your speed drops significantly below your contracted plan tier or fluctuates heavily, run diagnostic tests across different hours. The SpeedWatch Android app automates this with 24/7 background audits and official legal PDF dispute certificates.
Bufferbloat occurs when your router queues excessive packets during active downloads or uploads, causing severe latency spikes (lag). Even with 500 Mbps download, high bufferbloat creates rubber-banding in competitive games and frozen video on Zoom calls.
Wi-Fi is subject to radio frequency interference from neighboring routers, physical wall attenuation, and protocol overhead. Ethernet provides a dedicated full-duplex shielded copper connection, typically delivering 20% to 50% faster real throughput with near-zero packet loss.
A single 4K HDR video stream requires 25 Mbps continuous bandwidth. For households with multiple screens, smart TVs, and remote workers, 100–300 Mbps is recommended. For gaming, ping latency under 30 ms and jitter under 3 ms matter much more than raw download speed.
